Designer

Chris Cimer

Furniture designer and maker working between design, technical development and the workshop.

After more than a decade working within the British luxury interiors and bespoke furniture industry, I established my own practice to develop furniture through my own design language.

Each piece begins with proportion and purpose, then develops through material, construction and detail. I design furniture that can be made properly, used naturally and kept for years.
