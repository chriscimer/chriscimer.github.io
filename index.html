The designer

Chris Cimer

I grew up surrounded by timber, tools and workshops.

Long before I designed furniture, I learned how it was made. My family has worked with wood for generations, and craftsmanship has always been part of everyday life.

After moving to Britain, I spent more than fifteen years working across bespoke joinery, furniture production and high-end residential interiors in London. That experience shaped the way I think about proportion, construction, material and detail.

Today, every piece begins with a clear structure, then proportion, then material. I design each object myself and develop it for production in our workshop in Poland.

My goal has never been to create more furniture.
Only furniture worth keeping.

Designed by Chris Cimer. Crafted in Poland.

Process

From idea to object.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    Every project begins with the space, the brief and the intended use.

  2. 02

    Design

    Form, proportion, material direction and bespoke details are developed as one complete object.

  3. 03

    Technical development

    Every component is resolved through drawings, construction detailing and material specification.

  4. 04

    Craftsmanship

    Precise machine work is combined with hand finishing, material judgement and workshop experience.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Pieces are made to order, inspected and prepared for delivery to clients worldwide.
