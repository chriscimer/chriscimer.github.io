Designer

Chris Cimer

Furniture designer and maker working between design, technical development and making.

I grew up surrounded by timber, tools and workshops.

Long before I designed furniture, I learned how it was made. My family has worked with wood for generations, and craftsmanship has always been part of everyday life.

After moving to Britain, I spent more than fifteen years working across bespoke joinery, furniture production and high-end residential interiors in London. That experience shaped the way I think about proportion, construction, material and detail.

Today, every piece begins with a clear structure, followed by proportion and material. I design each object myself and develop it for production in my workshop in Poland.
